# Bærheim Golfklubb Working Budget — Hjemmebane 2027

## Quote Basis

This budget is based on the email offer from Golf Management dated 29 July 2026 for an event in the first week of August 2027, with a planned start around 11:00.

The offer states:

- Recommended field of approximately 70–80 players; maximum capacity stated as 150.
- Green fee, tournament setup, and shotgun start: **NOK 800 per player**.
- Venue assistance with start lists, longest-drive and closest-to-pin equipment, and result lists.
- Dinner: **NOK 600 plus VAT per person**, minimum 40 guests.
- Optional baguette, chocolate, and soft-drink package: **NOK 110 plus VAT per person**.
- Range balls: **NOK 60 for 30 balls**.
- Rental set: **NOK 200**, treated as a participant-paid extra and excluded from the event budget.
- Function room and projector are available. The exact minimum event or dinner spend is not stated.
- No deposit. Final invoicing is based on actual consumption, with one-month payment terms.
- Final commitment is needed approximately one month before the event. Same-day cancellations and no-shows are invoiced.
- Alcohol can be brought into the function room and onto the course. Mineral water, coffee, and other non-alcoholic drinks must be purchased from the venue.
- A venue golf cart can be borrowed without charge for on-course beverage service.
- Terraces and outdoor furniture are available for company stands.

## What Each Place Costs

The quoted and participant-linked budget cost is approximately:

`NOK 800 golf + NOK 750 dinner + NOK 137.50 food package + NOK 60 range + NOK 100 dinner drinks = NOK 1,847.50 per participant`

The fully allocated average cost is:

`NOK 177,122 / 72 = approximately NOK 2,460 per participant`

Company places should be described as included in sponsorship packages, not free. Sponsor revenue must cover both the direct cost of company representatives and shared event expenses.

## Cost Controls

- Ask for one VAT-inclusive package price covering golf, food package, dinner, room, projector, coffee, and water.
- Keep rental clubs as a participant-paid extra.
- Make range balls optional if sponsor income is below target.
- Use the venue's tournament equipment and outdoor furniture rather than renting duplicates.
- Require all prizes to be non-cash items supplied directly by sponsors.
- Confirm sponsor commitments before the one-month venue commitment.
- Keep the 10% contingency untouched until the final consumption-based invoice is received.
