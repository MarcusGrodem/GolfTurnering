The expected field is **approximately 80–90 players**, with 90 used as the working case in Excel. The 120-person figure in Stavanger Golfklubb's email only describes terrace service capacity and is not used as the attendance assumption. The club has offered **Tuesday 3 August** or **Friday 6 August 2027** as possible dates. Both avoid the club's Thursday member day and are compatible with the stated shotgun restriction, although neither is the Thursday originally requested.

Stavanger Golfklubb gave an approximate starting budget of **NOK 144,000**, dependent on menu choice. The email does not itemize how much of this is golf, tournament administration, course access, food, facilities, or VAT. The workbook therefore records it as one combined estimate and does not add dinner a second time.

With the same **NOK 140,000 sponsorship target** and **NOK 900 student fee** used in the Sola model, 67 paying students provide NOK 60,300 in student revenue. This leaves a projected funding gap of **NOK 8,150**. A fee of approximately **NOK 1,022** is required to break even in the 90-player case.

At the lower 80-player estimate, a 25% company share gives 20 company players and 60 paying students. With start packages and the same other assumptions, the working total becomes **NOK 206,800**, and the break-even student fee after NOK 140,000 sponsorship becomes approximately **NOK 1,113**.
