# Hjemmebane: Start Here

**Last context review:** 2026-08-03

## One-Sentence Brief

Hjemmebane is a yearly, one-day summer golf and networking event at Sola GK that connects students and recent graduates with Stavanger-area employers through an 18-hole scramble, dinner, afterparty, profiles, recruitment discovery, and sponsor participation.

## Current Priority

Validate and deliver the first-year event. Registration, payment, profiles, sponsor discovery, company access, team allocation, event information, administration, and event-day readiness take priority over next-year growth features.

## Confirmed Foundations

- The selected event and website name is **Hjemmebane**.
- Primary audience: active students and people who graduated within the previous two years, especially within business, finance, economics, and related subjects.
- Students do not need a Stavanger connection to participate.
- Working capacity: 72 players, approximately 54 students and 18 company representatives.
- Format: 18-hole scramble, shotgun start, teams of three or four, gross and net results.
- Daytime golf is followed by dinner and an afterparty.
- Students pay; companies participate through sponsor packages and invoices.
- The event aims for zero profit.
- The website supports Norwegian and English and must work well on mobile and desktop.
- Students can create profiles before payment. Successful payment confirms participation immediately when capacity is available.
- Authorized sponsor users can browse confirmed students and access deliberately shared documents during the defined access period.
- Company favorites are private from students, personal to each company user, and used only as a secondary team-allocation preference after handicap balance.
- Sola GK or GolfBox remains the operational source of truth for official golf data unless a different arrangement is approved.
- Marcus Grude Grodem is the sole administrator and final approver for the first release.

## Selected Visual Direction

The initial identity is **Kystfairway**: grounded, sociable, and ambitious, with roughly 60% golf and 40% professional networking.

- Primary: Fairway green `#18543F`.
- Surface: Scorecard `#F4F6F0`.
- Text: Ink `#17231E`.
- Action accent: Flag coral `#DC604D`.
- Supporting colors: Fjord `#7FA9B2` and Medal `#D1A647`.
- Display type: Bricolage Grotesque.
- Body and UI type: Archivo.
- Working logo: an `H` whose right stem becomes a golf flag.

See `website/BRAND_IDENTITY.md` for complete usage guidance and `website/COLOR_PALETTES.md` only when comparing alternative directions.

## Budget Baseline

- Sola GK fixed venue cost: NOK 17,000.
- Green fee assumption: NOK 900 per player.
- Food assumption: NOK 400–500 per person, with NOK 450 used as the midpoint.
- Prizes must be non-cash and sponsor-supplied; no prize expense is budgeted.
- Zip / Jåttå Bryggeri is a prospective afterparty partner that may provide venue access and drinks at purchase cost. This is not confirmed and no savings are booked yet.

Use `budget/sola-gk-budget.md` for the full assumptions and calculated scenario.
