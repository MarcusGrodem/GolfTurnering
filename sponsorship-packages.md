# Hjemmebane Sponsorship Packages

## Purpose

Hjemmebane is a neutral meeting place where students and several Stavanger-area employers can build relationships through golf, dinner, and informal networking.

Sponsors receive valuable participation and visibility, but no company should appear to own or host the event for the others. The top-tier sponsors are therefore called **Main Partners**. A Main Partner is one of several possible principal supporters; the company name should not be incorporated into the Hjemmebane name, and wording such as “Hjemmebane presented by [Company]” should not be used.

The working event budget is approximately **NOK 177,000**. The first sponsorship milestone is **NOK 140,000**, which should allow the participation fee for approximately 54 students to remain around **NOK 800–900**, subject to final supplier prices.

## Package Overview

| Package | Price | Company participants | Role |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Supporting Partner | NOK 15,000 | 1 | Entry package |
| Company Partner | NOK 30,000 | 2 | Mid-tier package |
| Main Partner | NOK 50,000 | 4 | One of the event's main sponsors |

Company participation in golf, food, dinner, and the evening program is included in the package price. These places should not be described as free because the package covers both the direct participant costs and a contribution toward the shared event costs.

## Supporting Partner — NOK 15,000

The Supporting Partner package is intended for smaller companies that want to participate in the network without making a large commitment.

Included benefits:

- One company representative participating in golf, food, dinner, and the evening program
- Company profile on the Hjemmebane website
- Logo in the shared partner section on the website and relevant event materials
- Access to student profiles and documents that students have deliberately shared with verified companies during the defined access period
- Shared recognition during the prize ceremony
- Opportunity to provide a non-cash prize
- Social-media acknowledgement before or after the event

**Positioning:** Participate in Hjemmebane and meet students naturally by playing alongside them.

## Company Partner — NOK 30,000

The Company Partner package should be the primary package for most participating employers.

It includes all Supporting Partner benefits, plus:

- Two company representatives participating in the event
- Representatives normally placed on separate teams to create broader student contact
- Company stand before the shotgun start
- More prominent website profile and logo placement
- Participation in structured dinner seating
- Recognition by the host during the opening and prize ceremony
- A three-minute company presentation during the structured speed-dating session
- Branding connected to one hole, activity, or tournament competition
- Opportunity to feature relevant internships, graduate roles, or other career opportunities on the company profile
- A short post-event summary with attendance and feedback figures

Possible branded competitions or activities include:

- Closest to the pin
- Longest drive
- Straightest drive
- Scorecard
- Refreshment station

**Positioning:** Build relationships with students throughout the entire day.

### Speed-Dating Presentation

Each Company Partner and Main Partner receives up to three minutes to introduce the company to the students during a structured speed-dating session. The presentation should be concise and student-relevant, for example covering what the company does, which types of talent it seeks, and relevant internships, graduate roles, or career opportunities.

The organizer controls the format, order, timing, and transitions so every participating company receives a clear and equal presentation window. The three-minute limit includes any video or other prepared material.

## Main Partner — NOK 50,000

Each Main Partner receives the strongest visibility and helps make the student experience possible, but does not own, present, or control Hjemmebane. More than one company may participate as a Main Partner.

It includes all Company Partner benefits, plus:

- Four company representatives participating in the event
- Premium stand position
- Largest logo size within the partner hierarchy
- Recognition as a **Main Partner** on the website and event materials
- Additional recognition from the host during the welcome and prize ceremony
- Branding connected to one significant event element, such as dinner, the welcome reception, or a main competition
- Dedicated company feature on the website and social media
- Early opportunity to renew the Main Partner package for the following event
- Selection of professional event photographs for approved company use
- A short, student-relevant stage contribution

### Stage Contribution

The stage contribution should provide value to students and the wider network rather than function as a conventional company advertisement. A recommended format is a five-minute host-led conversation followed by up to five minutes of audience questions.

Possible themes include:

- Why Stavanger needs young talent
- What students should know about building a career in the region
- Three things the representative wishes they had known at the beginning of their career
- A young employee describing their first years in working life
- A brief local industry outlook relevant to students

Alternatives to an individual presentation include:

- Opening a short panel involving other companies and a student
- Presenting one of the main tournament prizes
- Welcoming participants to dinner without delivering a company pitch
- Introducing a short student–company networking activity

The contribution should be tightly moderated and should not exceed ten minutes in total.

**Positioning:** Help make the complete Hjemmebane student experience possible.

## What Sponsors Are Supporting

The primary value is meaningful contact with approximately 54 students across a full day, rather than logo exposure alone. Sponsors can meet students through:

- Mixed student and company scramble teams
- Pre-start stands and introductions
- Several hours of conversation during the golf round
- Structured dinner seating
- The prize ceremony and evening program
- Company and student profiles on the Hjemmebane website

A useful way to explain the financial contribution is:

> Your partnership helps cover golf, food, dinner, and event operations so students can participate for approximately NOK 800–900 instead of bearing the full event cost of more than NOK 3,000 each.

## Partnership Guardrails

Hjemmebane should remain useful and welcoming to every participating company and student. Packages must therefore not include:

- Naming rights to Hjemmebane or wording such as “presented by [Company]”
- Control over the event program, participant selection, dinner seating, or team allocation
- Guaranteed placement with particular students
- Guaranteed interviews, hires, applications, or other recruitment results
- Unrestricted access to student CVs, documents, or personal information
- Industry exclusivity unless it is separately evaluated and explicitly agreed
- Long or repeated company presentations
- Branding so dominant that other partners appear to be guests at one company’s event

Student information must only be available to verified company users, for a defined recruitment purpose and access period, and only where the student has deliberately consented to sharing it.

## Optional In-Kind Partnerships

The following partnerships can sit outside the three cash packages and should normally reduce an actual event cost. They should not automatically include a company player place.

- **Prize Partner:** provides products, gift cards, or experiences as non-cash prizes
- **Afterparty Partner:** provides a venue, beverages, services, or a cash contribution
- **Photography Partner:** covers some or all of the event photographer cost
- **Transport Partner:** provides or funds participant transport
- **Welcome Pack Partner:** supplies a useful, good-quality participant item

The cash value and fair value of in-kind contributions should be recorded separately in the budget.

## First-Year Pricing Recommendation

Use the NOK 15,000, NOK 30,000, and NOK 50,000 prices for the first event. An initial sales mix of one Main Partner, one Company Partner, and four Supporting Partners produces NOK 140,000 in cash sponsorship and includes ten company participants.

The tournament has capacity for up to 18 company participants. The remaining places should not be promised or described as free capacity; they may be allocated through additional packages or individually negotiated agreements while respecting the event budget and overall participant limit.

Prices can be reconsidered after the first event once Hjemmebane has attendance figures, photographs, testimonials, sponsor feedback, and evidence of renewal interest.
