# Design Context

## Target Audience

Hjemmebane serves active students and recent graduates, primarily within business, finance, and economics, alongside Stavanger-area employers in banking, consulting, finance, and related industries.

## Primary Use Cases

- Students create profiles, pay for participation, discover companies, and prepare for the tournament.
- Company representatives present their business, browse participating students, and prepare for networking and scramble teams.
- The organizer manages registration, payments, participants, sponsors, teams, and event-day information.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, sociable, and ambitious. The experience should feel approximately 60% golf and 40% professional networking: credible enough for established employers, but informal and energetic enough to make students want to participate. It must avoid generic startup styling and overly traditional country-club aesthetics.

## Viewing Context

The public website is viewed mainly on phones and laptops before and during a summer daytime event. The authenticated product areas should prioritize clarity, speed, accessibility, and practical event information.
